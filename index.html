Questions, answered

How much does an artist photoshoot cost in Dubai?

My sessions start at AED 1,300 for an Artist Promo Session (2 hours, 10 edited photos). An Artist Portfolio is AED 1,500, and a full EPK & Live Show package starts from AED 4,500. All prices are listed above, with no hidden fees.

What is an EPK and do I need one?

An EPK (electronic press kit) is the package promoters, venues and labels ask for before booking you: professional portraits, live shots and short video. If you’re performing in Dubai and want more (and better-paid) gigs, it’s the single best investment in your brand.

How fast will I get my photos?

Edited photos are delivered within 72 hours for promo and portfolio sessions, and within one week for EPK & Live Show packages. You also get the full shoot on USB.

Where do you shoot?

Anywhere in Dubai and across the UAE — studios, clubs and live venues, rooftops, urban locations or the desert. We pick the setting that fits your sound and brand.

Do you photograph live shows and gigs?

Live performance is my specialty. Stage light, movement and crowd energy are exactly what a press kit needs to feel real.
