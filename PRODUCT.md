## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (user-confirmed). Hosted on GitHub Pages from `main`, CNAME `estrela.photo`. v2 redesign lives in `/v2/` until approved; v1 preserved on branch `v1-backup`.

## Users

Musicians, DJs, and performers based in or moving through Dubai / UAE who need professional visual assets — press kits (EPK), portfolios, promo shots, live-show coverage — to pitch promoters, labels, venues, and grow their brand. They evaluate on visual proof (the photos themselves) and book fast, usually via WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Estrela Photography (estrela.photo) is Javier Estrela's photography business site. Its job is to convert Dubai-based artists into booked shooting sessions. Success = inquiries/bookings (WhatsApp first, email form second). Owner relocates to Dubai on 2026-09-25; the site must be client-ready before then, with marketing (Instagram, ads) to follow.

## Positioning

Not a generalist photographer: a specialist in artist branding — "I don't just take photos; I build your brand." Full-stack visual assets for performers: high-end portraits + live performance shots + EPK material, delivered fast (72h for sessions).

## Operating Context

Dubai music/nightlife scene: DJs, live bands, performers pitching venues and promoters. Business closes over WhatsApp in this market (user-confirmed primary CTA). Pricing is public and in AED.

## Product Principles

1. The photography is the argument — show the work big; copy supports, never competes.
2. Zero friction to booking: WhatsApp one tap away from anywhere on the page.
3. Specialist positioning over generalist reach: everything speaks to performers, not "photography for everyone".
4. Dubai-first: location, currency (AED), market habits (WhatsApp) are explicit, load-bearing facts.
5. Fast and light: static site, no build, top Core Web Vitals — performance is part of the brand promise.
