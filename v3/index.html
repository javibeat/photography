Zone VField notes

Field notes

How much does an artist photoshoot cost in Dubai?

Sessions start at AED 1,300 for an Artist Promo Session (2 hours, 10 edited photos). An Artist Portfolio is AED 1,500, and a full EPK & Live Show package starts from AED 5,500. All prices are public, with no hidden fees.

What is an EPK and do I need one?

An EPK (electronic press kit) is the package promoters, venues and labels ask for before booking an artist: professional portraits, live shots and short video. For performers in Dubai it is the single best investment in their brand.

How fast will I get my photos?

Edited photos are delivered within 72 hours for promo and portfolio sessions, and within two weeks for EPK & Live Show packages. You also get the full shoot on USB.

Where do you shoot?

Anywhere in Dubai and the UAE: clubs and venues, studios, urban locations or the desert. We pick the setting that fits your sound and brand.

Do you photograph live shows and gigs?

Yes. Live performance coverage is a specialty: stage light, crowd energy and the moments that make a promoter book you.

How do I book a session?

The fastest way is WhatsApp: +971 58 532 4519. Tell me what you do and when you need the photos, or use the form below.
